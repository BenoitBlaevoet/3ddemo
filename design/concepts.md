# Stylized Lab: Product Concepts (Phase 0)

Criteria: bright colors, simple shapes, light, fun, young, easy to use, short slogan. Must read as a single hero object.

Slogans and logos are added in code/SVG, not in the generated image. AI text rendering is unreliable, and live text stays editable and animatable.

## 1. POPPO: sparkling fruit soda can
- **Slogan**: "Crack it. Pop it."
- **Shape**: short, chubby can with a rounded top. Easiest shape for image-to-3D.
- **Palette**: cherry red + lemon yellow + white
- **Krea prompt**:
  > anime cel-shaded product illustration of a short chubby soda can with rounded edges, cherry red and lemon yellow, bubbles and fizz splashing, thick black ink outlines, two-tone hard shadows, flat bold colors, 3/4 view, centered, plain sky blue background, speed lines, playful youthful energy, 90s anime commercial style, clean, no text

## 2. BLIP: pebble wireless earbuds case
- **Slogan**: "Tap. Vibe. Repeat."
- **Shape**: smooth pebble case, lid half-open, two bean-shaped buds
- **Palette**: mint green + hot pink + white
- **Krea prompt**:
  > anime cel-shaded product illustration of a round pebble-shaped wireless earbuds case, lid half open, two small bean-shaped earbuds floating out, mint green and hot pink, music notes and sparkles, thick black ink outlines, two-tone hard shadows, flat bold colors, 3/4 view, centered, plain lilac background, playful youthful energy, 90s anime commercial style, clean, no text
